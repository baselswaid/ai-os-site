ai-os privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

ai-os is a private application used by one person only: its owner and developer, Basel Swaid. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's explicit permission, ai-os reads the owner's own Gmail messages and Google Calendar events.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the owner with reminders, summaries and drafts for his own use. Drafts are never sent without the owner's explicit approval.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored only on a private server controlled by the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Content may be processed by an AI model provider solely to produce the owner's summaries and drafts.

Google user data

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time from his Google Account's third-party access settings.

Contact

Questions: contact the owner through the Google account associated with this app.
